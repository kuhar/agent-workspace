# Trace an engineering decision

Find the originating change or proposal and the discussion that explains the choice. Follow later revisions or decisions if they changed the answer. Cite the decisive commit, review comment, issue or design note. A closed PR establishes an outcome. Attribute a reason only when the record supports it.

When the question also asks whether an alternative would work today, check the relevant path on the requested or current revision. Identify which constraints come from implementation and which come from policy or a tradeoff the maintainers accepted.

Lead with the answer. Attribute the recorded rationale, label any inference and say what remains unknown when the record is silent.
